# For Vendors - Sales One-Pager

Copy and structure for the vendor pitch. Doubles as the source for a future
`/for-vendors` route. Pitch and pricing follow [STRATEGY.md](./STRATEGY.md);
proof comes from [LEAD-TRACKING.md](./LEAD-TRACKING.md).

## Headline

Get found by couples actually getting married in Newport - and keep every lead.

## The problem (their words)

The Knot and WeddingWire cost $5,000-15,000/yr for a featured listing, lock you
into a 12-month contract, and share the leads they send you with your
competitors. You pay thousands for leads other vendors answer too.

## The offer

Married in Newport is a local, curated, couple-first Newport wedding site.

- Direct leads, never resold. An inquiry through your page goes to you, not a
  shared pool.
- Local and curated, not a national directory dump. Newport couples, Newport
  vendors.
- No 12-month lock. Stay because it works.
- A real report. Monthly views, clicks, and leads for your listing - see exactly
  what you're paying for.

## Pricing

| Tier | Price | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | Free | Listed in the directory, standard placement. |
| Featured | ~$99/mo | Top-of-category placement, enhanced profile, inquiry form, monthly lead report. |
| Venue Premium | ~$250/mo | Everything in Featured, priority across venue surfaces, richer gallery. |

No contract. Cancel anytime.

## The ROI math

A Newport wedding booking is worth thousands - venues commonly $15,000+. Featured
at ~$1,200/yr pays for itself with a single booking, often many times over. The
question isn't cost; it's whether you want the couples already on the site.
