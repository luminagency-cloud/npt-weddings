# Roadmap

One sequence so every plan in the repo lines up. Each phase gates the next. Do
not start a later phase to escape the work of an earlier one. Strategy behind
this lives in [STRATEGY.md](./STRATEGY.md).

## Phase 1 - Launch the directory (now)

Goal: a credible, live, couple-first directory in one market (Newport / RI).

- Execute the launch punch list in [TODO.md](./TODO.md) and the checklist in
  [DEPLOY.md](./DEPLOY.md).
- Success = real inventory depth, images on venue pages, tight copy, clean
  production QA, healthy indexing.
- No monetization work here. Do not block launch on Stripe, paid upgrades, or
  vendor self-service.

## Phase 2 - Build demand and prove leads (next)

Goal: demonstrable couple traffic and vendor leads, so featured listings become
sellable.

- Grow SEO / content (planning + Newport guides already do this well).
- Instrument lead tracking: capture and attribute vendor profile views, clicks,
  and contact / lead events. This is the sales proof for Phase 3.
- Optional demand lever: the planning app (see [APP-FUNNEL.md](./APP-FUNNEL.md))
  can start here as a cheap, genuinely-AI couple tool that feeds traffic - only
  if it does not slow Phase 1 or 2.

Gate to Phase 3: you can show a vendor real, recent lead volume for their
category.

## Phase 3 - Monetize vendors

Goal: recurring vendor revenue in the first market.

- Ship the minimum paid mechanics: featured / priority placement, enhanced
  profile. Stripe plus a lean self-serve or manual billing flow. (Env vars
  already reserved.)
- Sell against The Knot: direct leads, local curation, no 12-month lock, priced
  as an obvious yes. Pricing in [STRATEGY.md](./STRATEGY.md).
- Convert claimed listings to featured. Keep basic listings free for inventory
  and SEO.

Gate to Phase 4: Newport is at or near its run-rate and the sales motion is
repeatable.

## Phase 4 - Expand markets and app tie-in

Goal: replicate the proven market playbook.

- Stand up the next market as a content + inventory + local-sales build, not new
  engineering.
- Route app couples to covered markets; per-market directory tie-in in the app.
- Repeat. Expansion pace is limited by sales / content time, not code.

## Not on this roadmap

- Couple subscriptions or paid couple tiers.
- Rebuilding The Knot's feature breadth.
- App features beyond what earns couple attention cheaply.
